{# Plain words (#884 G7). The meaning of each point is unchanged, so CONSENT_VERSION is not bumped here; a change of meaning would need one (careagents/tester_terms.py). #}

Short answers to the questions that matter. It takes 30 seconds.

  • These are your records, and you have a right to them. You are getting your own health records and sending them to a tool you chose. CareAgents is not your doctor or a hospital, so some rules work differently than you may expect.
  • Where your records live: with HealthClaw, in a private space just for you. Your name and details are taken out before your assistant reads a record, and every look is logged. CareAgents keeps only your account, not your health records.
  • What the AI sees: your records with your name and details taken out, only when a question needs them. Your records are never used to train AI and never sold.
  • The AI can be wrong, and your records may be missing things. Don't use either in place of your official records or your doctor's advice.
  • Leaving: you can leave yourself, any time, right here. Disconnect stops new records coming in and keeps what's already here. Delete removes the records for good and tells you how many were deleted. We keep a list of when your records were looked at, with no health details. Deleting your records doesn't delete that list. Stuck? Email contactus@healthclaw.io.
