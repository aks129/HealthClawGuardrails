CareAgents is an assistant that reads health records and drafts paperwork, and you approve anything it sends. It is not a doctor, and it does not give medical advice.

During the beta you use made-up sample records, not your own. It takes about 15 minutes.

  1. Sign up with your email.
  2. Pick the made-up sample records and ask a question.
  3. Try an approval. Ask it to fill in an intake form, then check it and approve it.

Watch the short demo videos to see each step first.
