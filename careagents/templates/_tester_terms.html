Tester terms

  • This is a test version. Things will break, and features may change or go away. We may pause your use of your own records, for example while we fix a problem.
  • It is not medical advice, and not for emergencies. In an emergency, call 911.
  • Answers can be wrong. Check anything important with your doctor or nurse before you act on it.
  • Your records: you bring them in, from your patient portal through Fasten Health, or from a file or device you add. HealthClaw keeps them, and your chats, in a private space for your account. The AI comes from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or Groq. It sees your records with your name and details taken out, but it gets what you type exactly as you type it.
  • Texting uses made-up records only. Once your assistant uses your own records, a text to it tells you to open the app instead of answering.
  • You can stop at any time. Disconnect stops new records and keeps the ones already here. Delete removes those records, your chats and anything waiting for your OK, for good. Deleting your account also removes your email and passkey. Our log of when your records were looked at stays. It has no health details in it.
  • Taking part is up to you. You can say no, or stop later, without giving a reason.
  • Tell us what happens. Use the feedback link in the app, or email contactus@healthclaw.io. Please leave health details out.
  • These terms may change. If they do, we will ask you to accept them again before your assistant uses your records.
