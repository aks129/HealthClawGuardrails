How it keeps you safe

Most health-AI demos ask you to trust them. This one shows its work. Your name and details are taken out before your assistant reads a record, every look is logged, your records are kept apart from everyone else's, and nothing goes out without your OK. The open-source layer underneath (HealthClaw) checks itself on each of these and gives itself a grade from A to F, live, in public. See today’s grade.
